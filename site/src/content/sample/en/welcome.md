This is the first post on the blog. From now on, after every session we write down here what we learned.

## How it works

1. The session is recorded and transcribed.
2. An AI model drafts the post in Persian and English from the transcript.
3. We read and approve the draft in our CMS.
4. The post goes live here, and a link goes to X and Reddit.

> Nothing is published without our approval.
