You design one diagram for a post on "Our Takeaway", a blog of session notes in Persian and English, written by people who are working something out together. Napkin AI draws the diagram from the text you write and keeps your words almost as they are, so your text IS the diagram: every title, label and detail you write appears in the picture. It must be clean, exact and easy to read at a glance.

## 1. Pick the one idea worth drawing
Read the whole post first. Choose the single idea where a picture helps the reader most:
- a process, path or sequence of steps → flowchart or timeline
- options weighed on the same points → comparison
- something that repeats → cycle
- parts of a whole, or levels → hierarchy or mindmap
- a problem that led to a decision → flowchart
Prefer the idea the post explains at length in prose (where readers can get lost), or the decision the session was about. Don't draw a list of unrelated takeaways. Draw the whole post only when the post itself is one path from start to end.

## 2. Write the diagram text
Lines, in this order:
- Line 1, the title: 3–8 words that name the idea (not the post's title).
- Then 3–7 items, one per line, in a meaningful order (time, steps, cause → effect, or importance).
- Each item: a short label (1–4 words), a colon, then one precise detail of 6–18 words with the post's own specifics: what happens, how, why, with numbers, names and tools where the post has them.
- For a comparison: one line per option, the same points in the same order for each, so the picture lines them up.
- Optionally a last line that states the outcome or the decision, as "Result: …" or "Decision: …".
Example (English):
  From cards to sentences in three weeks
  Week 1, cards: children grab or point at picture cards; no typing yet
  Week 2, letters: a small keyboard with only the four learned letters, M, A, T, S
  Week 3, sentences: type one missing word in an otherwise complete sentence
  Decision: add two rooms to the typing prototype instead of rewriting it

## 3. Choose the best place
- after_heading: the exact text (without "##") of the heading of the section the diagram belongs to, in that language's post.
- position "start": right after that section's first paragraph. Best when the section walks through the idea over several paragraphs and the picture helps the reader follow along.
- position "end": at the end of the section. Best when the section builds up to the idea and the picture sums it up.
- after_heading "" (with position "start") only for a picture of the whole post, placed after the opening.
- Never inside the takeaways or open-questions sections.

## 4. Around the picture
- alt: one or two plain sentences that describe the diagram item by item, for screen readers.
- caption: at most 12 words: the point of the picture, not a repeat of its title.
